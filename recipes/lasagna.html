Lasagna

Description

This classic homemade lasagna features rich, savory layers of seasoned ground beef, vibrant marinara sauce, and a creamy three-cheese blend. It represents comfort food at its finest, balancing tender pasta sheets with a golden, bubbly top layer of melted mozzarella and parmesan cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground beef
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 cups marinara or tomato sauce
  • 9 to 12 classic lasagna noodles
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese, divided
  • 1 tsp dried Italian seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C) and boil lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  2. Cook ground beef and diced onion in a skillet until browned; drain excess fat.
  3. Add minced garlic, seasoning, and marinara sauce to the skillet; simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Combine ricotta cheese, egg, 1/4 cup parmesan, and a pinch of salt in a bowl.
  5. Spread a layer of sauce in a 9x13 dish, then alternate layers of noodles, ricotta, mozzarella, and meat sauce.
  6. Cover with foil and bake for 45 minutes; remove foil and bake 5 more minutes until golden.
  7. Cool for 15 to 20 minutes before slicing to let the layers set.
